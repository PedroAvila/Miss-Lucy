# 5-Minutes Fluency Drill — Summary

> **Objetivo:** automatizar 6 chunks de habito/habilidad/modales de probabilidad y combinarlos con marcadores de tiempo.

---

## Chunk 1 — `I tend to + verbo`

**Uso:** habito (*habit*)

| Chunk | Complemento |
|---|---|
| I tend to | wake up early |
| I tend to | have a big breakfast |
| I tend to | drink tea |
| I tend to | get nervous — before meetings |

---

## Chunk 2 — `I'm used to + verbo-ing`

**Uso:** accion familiar (*familiar action*)

| Chunk | Complemento |
|---|---|
| I'm used to | getting up early |
| I'm used to | working — on my own |
| I'm used to | living — in the countryside |

> ⚠️ Ojo: despues de *used to* va **-ing**, no infinitivo.

---

## Chunk 3 — `I find it easy to + verbo`

**Uso:** habilidad natural (*natural skill*)

| Chunk | Complemento |
|---|---|
| I find it easy | to cook |
| I find it easy | to learn languages |
| I find it easy | to stay organised |

---

## Chunk 4 — `I might as well + verbo`

**Uso:** decision por falta de mejor opcion (*lack of a better option*)

| Chunk | Complemento |
|---|---|
| I might as well | stay in tonight |
| I might as well | finish the report now |
| I might as well | walk, it's not far |

---

## Chunk 5 — `I'd rather + verbo`

**Uso:** preferencia (*preference*)

| Chunk | Complemento |
|---|---|
| I'd rather | stay in — than go out |
| I'd rather | not talk about it |
| I'd rather | you called me later |

> ⚠️ Ojo: si el sujeto cambia (ej. "you"), *rather* va seguido de pasado simple, no infinitivo — "I'd rather you called me later", no "to call".

---

## Chunk 6 — `You'd better + verbo`

**Uso:** advertencia o consejo fuerte (*strong warning/advice*)

| Chunk | Complemento |
|---|---|
| You'd better | hurry up |
| You'd better | not be late |
| You'd better | check the weather first |

> ⚠️ Ojo: la negacion va antes del verbo — "you'd better not be late", no "you'd not better be late".

---

## Combinaciones

**Formula:** `marcador de tiempo` + `chunk` + `complemento` + `because` + `razon`

### Marcadores de tiempo
- At the moment
- Right now
- By and large
- Honestly
- To be fair
- All things considered

### Ejemplos

| Marcador | Chunk | Complemento | Razon |
|---|---|---|---|
| At the moment | I tend to | wake up early | — |
| Right now | I'm used to | living on my own | — |
| By and large | I find it easy | to stay organised | — |
| At the moment | I tend to | wake up early | because I'm very busy |
| By and large | I find it easy | to wake up early | because I go to bed early |
| Right now | I'm used to | going to bed early | because I'm on holiday |
| Honestly | I might as well | stay in tonight | because it's raining |
| To be fair | I'd rather | not talk about it | because it's still fresh |
| All things considered | You'd better | check the weather first | because it might rain |
