# Connectors

> **Meta:** Dominar estos conectores. No saber usarlos en conversaciones reales es un descuido — hay que practicarlos activamente hasta que salgan sin pensar.

## I mean
**Osea / Me refiero a**
- She's not very friendly. I mean, she's not rude, but she's just quiet.
- I mean, it's not that big a deal.

## On top of that
**Encima de eso** (en la mayoría de los casos se usa para quejarse)
- He was late. On top of that, he forgot the tickets.
- The hotel was expensive. On top of that, the room was dirty.

## Then again
**Aunque pensándolo bien / Aunque por otro lado**
- I might go to the party. Then again, I'm pretty tired.
- She seems nice. Then again, we just met her.

## At the end of the day
**Cuando todo está dicho y hecho / Al final de cuentas / Lo que realmente importa es**
- At the end of the day, you have to do what makes you happy.
- We argued a lot, but at the end of the day, we're still family.

## By the way (BTW)
**Por cierto / A propósito / Ya que estamos**
- By the way, did you finish that report?

## Besides
**Además de eso / Aparte de eso**
- I don't feel like going out. Besides, it's raining.
- I'm not hungry. Besides, we just ate.

## To be fair
**Para ser justos**
- She didn't help much. To be fair, she was feeling sick.
- He's always late. To be fair, he has three kids and a crazy job.

## In fact
**De hecho / En realidad / Es más**
- He's a great cook. In fact, he used to work in a restaurant.
- I'm not tired. In fact, I feel amazing.

## Anyway
**De todas formas / De todos modos**
- Anyway, as I was saying, we need to finish the project by next Friday.
- Anyway, I should get going.
- I didn't really like the movie, anyway.

## That said
**Aun así / Igual / De todos modos**
- He's very talented. That said, he still needs to practice more.
- I love living in the city. That said, sometimes I miss the quiet of the countryside.

## Practice

- I'm practicing Java with Spring Boot. When I say Java, I mean Java 25; in fact, 
it's an amazing version. That said, I still respect the older versions like Java 8.

- My dream is to live in Europe. In fact, I plan to travel to England next year. 
I mean, living and working in England. But at the end of the day, 
the key to making this happen is getting a sponsor for a work visa.

- Next year, I will travel to England. To be fair, it's not going to be easy. 
Besides, I need a sponsor to get my work visa.

- I really enjoy working with Java. In fact, I love C# just as much!

- I like working on backend logic. 
I mean, I really enjoy programming the business rules that make the whole system work
